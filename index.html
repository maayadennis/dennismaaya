01 — ABOUT

Scientific thinking. Practical delivery.

My background is in the lab, not the newsroom, which is why clients bring me the messy, technical stuff: datasets that need cleaning before they mean anything, findings that need structure, documentation that needs to survive someone else reading it. I organize it, analyze it, and write it up as a report with a methodology, results and a clear recommendation — not just a summary.
